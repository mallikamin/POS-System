## 1. What you asked for

You want online ordering for the takeaway, running alongside your existing EposNow system rather
than replacing it.

Customers order from your website. Orders arrive on a tablet in the shop. You accept or reject each
one and tell the customer how long it will take. Customers pay by card online, or cash on delivery.
Their choice.

EposNow stays exactly as it is for everything in-house. Nothing changes for your staff on the counter.
The two systems stay separate, as you asked.

## 2. What we will build

### The ordering website

- Your full menu online, with categories, item options and prices.
- Cart and checkout that works properly on a phone. That is where most orders come from.
- Card payment through Stripe, using the account you already have. Money goes straight to your bank.
- Cash on delivery as an alternative. The customer picks at checkout.
- Collection or delivery. For delivery we capture the address and apply your delivery area, delivery
  charge and minimum order.
- Order confirmation on screen and by email, including the time you quote.

Built on the second domain you already own. Your main information site is untouched.

### The order screen

- Opens as a link on any basic Android tablet. Nothing to install.
- New orders appear on screen as they come in, with an alert.
- **Accept or reject** each order with one tap.
- On accepting, set the lead time: 30 minutes, 45 minutes, whatever it is. The customer is told
  automatically.
- Orders stay on screen until they are done, so nothing gets missed on a busy night.
- Prints the order ticket to a receipt printer if you want one. We will set up the spare printer
  you have.

### Your reporting login

A separate web link, just for you:

- Sales for the day, week and month.
- Number of orders, and average order value.
- Best and worst selling items.
- Full order history, searchable by phone number, so you can see repeat customers and what they
  ordered before.

This is the backend you described from your previous restaurant. It covers online orders only. It does
not touch or read anything in EposNow.

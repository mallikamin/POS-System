## How to use this document

Walk it top to bottom, on production, signed in as the FZ admin. Fill in the **Result**
column as you go: `PASS`, or `FAIL` plus what you actually saw. Do not write PASS for a
step you skipped or reasoned about. A step that was not performed is `NOT RUN`.

Nothing in here is sent to Martin. When every row reads PASS, the reply to Martin gets
written from the summary at the bottom, and the partner meeting can be offered.

🔴 **Run this on a phone as well as a laptop.** M7 was a phone-only defect, and Martin's
one positive line was "on the laptop works perfect". A laptop-only pass proves half of it.

### Things to tell him rather than let him discover

1. **Charges sit outside the VAT** (F6). If FZ LLC needs VAT charged on delivery fees, say so and it becomes a follow-up.
2. **He adds Deliveroo and Keeta himself** on the Sales Channels screen, with their commission rates. They become POS tiles at once.
3. **The conversion belongs to the ingredient, not to the supplier.** One tomato can is 400 g regardless of who sells it. If two suppliers ship genuinely different can sizes for the same ingredient, that is a follow-up, not something the current build handles.
4. **Recipe costs are snapshots.** A parent recipe picks up a changed sub-recipe or ingredient cost the next time it is saved, not retroactively. This is deliberate: it stops a price rise from rewriting the history of what a dish used to cost.

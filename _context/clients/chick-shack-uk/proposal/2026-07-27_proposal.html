Online Ordering Website + Order Management

Proposal and Scope of Work

1. What you asked for

You want online ordering for the takeaway, running alongside your existing EposNow system rather than replacing it.

Customers order from your website. Orders arrive on a tablet in the shop. You accept or reject each one and tell the customer how long it will take. Customers pay by card online, or cash on delivery. Their choice.

EposNow stays exactly as it is for everything in-house. Nothing changes for your staff on the counter. The two systems stay separate, as you asked.

2. What we will build

The ordering website

  • Your full menu online, with categories, item options and prices.
  • Cart and checkout that works properly on a phone. That is where most orders come from.
  • Card payment through Stripe, using the account you already have. Money goes straight to your bank.
  • Cash on delivery as an alternative. The customer picks at checkout.
  • Collection or delivery. For delivery we capture the address and apply your delivery area, delivery charge and minimum order.
  • Order confirmation on screen and by email, including the time you quote.

Built on the second domain you already own. Your main information site is untouched.

The order screen

  • Opens as a link on any basic Android tablet. Nothing to install.
  • New orders appear on screen as they come in, with an alert.
  • Accept or reject each order with one tap.
  • On accepting, set the lead time: 30 minutes, 45 minutes, whatever it is. The customer is told automatically.
  • Orders stay on screen until they are done, so nothing gets missed on a busy night.
  • Prints the order ticket to a receipt printer if you want one. We will set up the spare printer you have.

Your reporting login

A separate web link, just for you:

  • Sales for the day, week and month.
  • Number of orders, and average order value.
  • Best and worst selling items.
  • Full order history, searchable by phone number, so you can see repeat customers and what they ordered before.

This is the backend you described from your previous restaurant. It covers online orders only. It does not touch or read anything in EposNow.

The £300 covers: the ordering website, checkout, Stripe connection, the order screen, your reporting login, loading your menu, connecting your domain, and setting up the tablet and printer.

Not included: Stripe's own card processing fees. Those are charged by Stripe directly to you at their standard rates, the same as any other website.

4. Support

Support is included in the monthly fee. It works the way you described:

  • WhatsApp. Message us and we will deal with it. No ticket system, no call centre.
  • Menu changes, price changes, new items, seasonal offers. Send them over and we make the change. No charge.
  • Anything broken, we fix it, priority.
  • Setup help. We will get the tablet working, connect the printer, sort the domain and help you configure Stripe. You do not need to figure any of it out yourself.

New features beyond what is listed in this document are quoted separately before any work starts. You will never get an unexpected bill.

5. What we need from you

  1. Your menu: items, prices, options and sizes. A photo of the printed menu or a spreadsheet is fine.
  2. Your FastHost login for the domain and hosting, so we can put the site up for you.
  3. Delivery rules: how far you deliver, delivery charge, and minimum order value.
  4. Opening hours, and whether you want customers to be able to order ahead for later.
  5. Your Stripe account connected. We will walk you through it, it takes a few minutes.
  6. Logo and any photos you want on the site.
  7. The spare tablet and printer you mentioned, so we can check they will do the job.

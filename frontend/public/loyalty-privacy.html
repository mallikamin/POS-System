Loyalty Card: Privacy and Terms

Last updated: 3 October 2026

This page covers the visit loyalty card you join by scanning the QR code on a restaurant bill or receipt, and the same card saved in Google Wallet.

1. Who runs the card

The restaurant whose name is on your card runs its loyalty programme and decides the reward. Sitara Infotech (Sitara Infotect Pvt Ltd, 5th Floor, Sitara Tower, New Civil Lines, Faisalabad 38000, Pakistan) provides the point-of-sale and loyalty system the restaurant uses, and issues the Google Wallet card on the restaurant's behalf.

Questions, or a request about your data: email amin@sitaratech.info, or ask at the restaurant's counter.

2. What we keep

  • Your mobile number. It is your card: it identifies you at the till.
  • Your name, only if you give it.
  • Your visits: the date of each paid bill counted on your card, and which bill it was.
  • Rewards you have used.

We do not ask for your email, address or date of birth, and we do not read anything else on your phone.

3. Why we keep it

Only to count your visits, show your progress, and give you your reward. If you add the card to Google Wallet, we update the visit count on it after each visit and Google Wallet may show you a notification.

4. Google Wallet

Adding the card to Google Wallet is optional. If you do, Google stores the card on your Google account. The card shows the restaurant's name, a masked version of your number, your visit count and a QR code of your number for scanning at the till. Google's handling of your Google account is covered by Google's own privacy policy. You can remove the card from Google Wallet at any time.

5. What we never do

  • We do not sell your data or share it with data brokers.
  • We do not use your card data for advertising.
  • Your number is never shown to other customers. The restaurant's staff see it so they can find your card.

6. How long we keep it

For as long as you are a member of the card. Ask us to delete it and we remove your number, name and visit history from the loyalty card within 30 days. Bills you paid stay in the restaurant's sales records, which it must keep for its accounts and taxes.

7. Your choices

You can ask for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, using the email above.

8. Card terms

  • A visit counts only when a bill is paid, once per bill.
  • The number of visits needed and the reward are shown on your card and at the restaurant. The restaurant may also limit how many visits count in one day.
  • Rewards have no cash value, cannot be exchanged for money and cannot be transferred to another number.
  • The card is free. Joining never costs anything.
  • The restaurant may change or end the programme.

9. Changes to this page

If we change this page, the date at the top changes too.

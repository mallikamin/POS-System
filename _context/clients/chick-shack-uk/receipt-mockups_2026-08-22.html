Two changes to the printed ticket

Nothing on the website changes. Customers still pick a tip at checkout and still pay the same amounts. The only thing that changes is what comes out of the kitchen printer when an order is accepted.

1. The tip stops being its own line. It is folded into Subtotal, then Platform Fee and Delivery print below it as now. The cash COLLECT figure does not change: the rider still brings back the full amount.

2. A Google review QR at the bottom of the slip, like the EposNow receipt. Same review link as the email we already send, so all reviews land in one place.

Every slip below is drawn to the same 48 columns the printer uses. Amounts are Imran's own example: £25.75 of food, £3.50 tip, 70p platform fee, £3.00 delivery to Garelochhead.

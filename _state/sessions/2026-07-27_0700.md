## Decisions

- **CP437, not UTF-8.** Every ticket carries a pound sign; UTF-8 `£` prints as two junk characters.
- **Server-side ESC/POS.** The tablet stays dumb; a printer quirk is fixed by a deploy, not by
  touching a device in Scotland.
- **No `python-escpos`.** It pulls Pillow and USB backends to drive printers directly, which we do
  not do. Eight commands, hand-rolled, tested.
- **The ticket endpoint is authenticated**, unlike everything else in `public.py`, because a ticket
  carries a name, phone and home address.
- **The test page has no backend.** If it called our API a failure could mean the API, the token,
  CORS or the printer. Static means a failure means one thing.
- **A PWA does not help printing** and was ruled out for that purpose — same sandbox, no TCP. It is
  still worth building for the order screen, where push notifications are most of the "Uber Eats
  tablet" feel.
